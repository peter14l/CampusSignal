The Problem

Great opportunities are getting lost in the noise.

On campus, event information lives everywhere — and nowhere. Students miss hackathons, workshops and internships not because they don’t care, but because the details never reach them in time.

WhatsApp groups

Announcements scroll away in minutes and get buried under hundreds of chats.

Paper posters

Pinned on boards and doors — easy to miss, and details are often unclear.

Email notices

Flooded inboxes mean important circulars go unread or unopened.

Word of mouth

Some students hear about events; many never do at all.

Missed deadlines

Registration often closes before students even know the event exists.

Students don’t miss events because they are lazy — they miss them because the information is scattered.

How a Student Uses It

From one tap to the application — in four easy steps.

STEP 1

Discover

Open the feed. Events are sorted to match your department, year and interests — or search any category anytime.

STEP 2

Understand

Tap any event to see dates, venue, who it’s for, and how to apply — all in one clean card.

STEP 3

Plan

Save events you like, add them to your phone calendar, and set a reminder before the deadline.

STEP 4

Apply

Open the application link in one tap, and keep everything you’ve applied to in a single place.

Our Design Thinking Journey

From listening to students, to a working app.

01 · EMPATHISE

Listen

We spoke with students and watched how campus events are shared — and how easily they get missed.

What we produced — notes from student conversations
02 · DEFINE

Focus

We framed one clear problem: scattered event information makes students miss real opportunities.

What we produced — one clear problem statement
03 · IDEATE

Imagine

We brainstormed many ideas and chose the strongest: a personal, easy-to-read campus event feed.

What we produced — a shortlist of ideas & the chosen one
04 · PROTOTYPE

Build

We designed every screen on paper, then built a real working version of the app.

What we produced — screen designs & a working app
05 · TEST

Refine

Students tried the app, gave honest feedback, and we improved the design around their habits.

What we produced — feedback notes & improvements

Every stage started with students — and ended with a change they could feel.
