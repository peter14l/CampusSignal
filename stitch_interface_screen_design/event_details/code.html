About the Event

Join us for the most anticipated tech event of the semester! Code for Campus is a 48-hour sprint where you'll build solutions for real-world university problems. Whether you're a seasoned developer or a first-year designer, there's a place for you to learn, build, and showcase your talent.

Mentors from top tech firms will be on-site to guide you. Free food, endless coffee, and exclusive swag for all participants. Grand prize includes $1000 in cloud credits and an interview loop with our sponsor companies.
