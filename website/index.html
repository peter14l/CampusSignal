THE PROBLEM VS THE SIGNAL

Built specifically to conquer campus notification fatigue.

University announcements at St. Xavier's are rich with world-class opportunities, but critical notices get buried under chat storms and degraded flyers.

The WhatsApp & Notice Board Chaos

How opportunities were missed before

  • Unsearchable Chat Flood: Vital hackathon & internship announcements get drowned in 500+ daily informal messages.
  • Compressed Paper Posters: Low-res flyers with tiny unclickable URLs and obscured registration deadlines.
  • Eligibility Blind Spots: Reading through dense PDFs just to check if your semester or degree qualifies.
  • No Native Reminders: Manually typing event dates into Google Calendar or forgetting until registration closes.

The CampusSignal Experience

Structured, personalized, and deadline-guaranteed

  • Multimodal Vision OCR: Ingest any flyer image or PDF; Gemini 2.5 extracts dates, links, eligibility, and venues in <1.2s.
  • Targeted 28-Programme Engine: Automatic matching against your exact SXUK UG/PG faculty and academic year.
  • Native Calendar Injection: 1-tap exact system alarm scheduling with proactive 24h & 2h countdown push alerts.
  • Drift SQLite Offline Cache: Instant 0ms cold start with full offline bookmarking and calendar access.
